Project Overview

What Edgecipline is, why it exists, and the problem it solves

What Is Edgecipline?

Edgecipline is an AI-powered trading journal and behavioral coaching platform built for retail traders. It combines trade logging, performance analytics, psychological pattern detection, and AI-generated coaching — all in one mobile-first application available on Android and the web.

The name says it all: Edge (your statistical trading advantage) + Discipline (the only thing standing between a trader and consistent profitability).
💡
Most traders don't fail because they lack a strategy. They fail because they can't follow one. Edgecipline exists to solve exactly that problem.

Frequently Asked Questions

70 questions across 12 categories — from first-time visitors to power users

A — Getting Started
Q1 What exactly is Edgecipline and how is it different from a regular trading journal?
A regular journal records what happened. Edgecipline is a behavioral coaching system built on top of a journal. It tracks mood, emotions, and confidence — and connects those directly to P&L. Then an AI coach analyzes patterns every morning and every week, telling you exactly what you're doing wrong and what it costs you. No spreadsheet does that.
Q2 I'm a complete beginner. Is this app for me?
Edgecipline is best for traders who have a strategy but struggle to execute it consistently. Beginners can absolutely use it to build disciplined habits from day one — which is far better than forming bad habits and trying to fix them later.
Q3 What markets does Edgecipline support?
MarketCoverage
Forex / GlobalCurrency pairs, indices, commodities, CFDs
Indian MarketsNSE/BSE Equity, F&O, Intraday, Delivery, BTST, Commodity
RoadmapUS Stocks, Crypto
Q4 Is Edgecipline a web app, mobile app, or both?
Both. Available as a Progressive Web App (browser) and native Android app. The Android app supports push notifications for the Morning Mentor — a key advantage over web-only journals. iOS is on the roadmap.
Q5 How long does it take to see useful insights?
  • 5–10 trades: Basic KPIs appear
  • 30–50 trades: Psychology correlations and setup performance emerge
  • 100+ trades: Full behavioral pattern detection and reliable mood-P&L correlation
Q6 Can I import old trades from a spreadsheet?
CSV/Excel bulk import is on the roadmap. Currently, use manual entry or AI screenshot extraction for past trades. You can upload historical screenshots from your broker platform.
B — Trade Logging
Q7 How do I log a trade?
Manual: Add Trade → fill in details + psychology tags → save. ~60–90 seconds.

AI Screenshot: Upload Trade → upload broker screenshot → AI pre-fills form → review + confirm. ~15–30 seconds.
Q8 What are "emotional tags" and why should I use them?
Tags describe your mental state: FOMO, Revenge, Fear, Greed, Impatient, Hesitant, Confident, Disciplined.

After 50+ trades, analytics shows the P&L difference between "Disciplined" trades and "Revenge" trades. For most traders, this single comparison is worth the entire subscription — it puts a dollar figure on emotional trading.
Q9 What is the "Would You Retake?" field?
After a trade exits, you answer: "If you could go back to the entry moment, would you take this trade again?" — regardless of win or loss.

Over time, this calibrates your instinct. If you say "Yes" to losing trades, your intuition is inversed. If you consistently say "No" to trades that lose — your gut is already ahead of your execution.
Q10 Can I edit or delete a trade after saving?
Yes. Find the trade in the Journal and use edit or delete. Editing updates all analytics immediately. Only correct genuine data entry errors — don't retroactively delete losing trades as it corrupts your analytics.
C — AI Screenshot Extraction
Q11 How does AI trade extraction work?
Upload a broker screenshot → AI uses OCR + Vision to detect broker type, read all trade data, extract symbol/direction/prices/P&L/timestamp, and pre-fill the form. Each field has a confidence score. You review, correct any errors, add psychology tags, and confirm.
Q12 Which broker platforms does the AI support?
Forex: MetaTrader 4/5, cTrader, TradingView, and most major statement formats.
Indian: Zerodha Kite, Groww, Upstox, Angel One, IIFL, Dhan, Fyers, 5Paisa, and growing. Manual correction is always available for any misread fields.
Q13 Can I upload multiple trades from one screenshot?
Yes. If the screenshot shows multiple closed trades (e.g., trade history page), AI detects and extracts all of them. You review each and confirm which to save.
D — Analytics
Q14 What is the "Discipline Score" and how is it calculated?
The % of trades confirmed through the Pre-Trade Checklist before entry.

Formula: (Disciplined Trades ÷ Total Trades) × 100

Above 80% = excellent. Below 50% = trading on impulse more than rules.
Q15 What is the "Repeated Mistakes Tracker"?
Ranks your self-tagged mistakes by total P&L cost. If you tagged "No Stop Loss" on 5 trades that lost ₹12,400, it shows at the top ranked by cost. The insight: fixing your #1 mistake typically recovers more P&L than finding a new strategy.
Q16 How does mood tracking connect to P&L?
Every trade has a mood rating (1–5). Analytics plots average P&L by mood level. Most traders discover they win significantly more when calm (mood 3–4) vs stressed (mood 1–2). Once visible in your own data, the motivation to trade with the right mindset becomes personal — not theoretical advice.
Q17 Can I filter analytics by time period or setup?
Yes. Filters available: Time period (Today/Week/Month/Last 3M/Custom), Setup/Strategy, Direction (Long/Short), Market (Forex/Indian).
E — Pre-Trade Checklist
Q18 Do I have to use the checklist before every trade?
No — it's not mandatory. But every trade taken without checklist confirmation is counted as "undisciplined" in your Discipline Score. Over time, your data will show the P&L difference between checklist-confirmed trades and impulse trades. For most traders, this difference makes the checklist a non-negotiable habit.
Q19 What if I score Moderate on the checklist — is it okay to take the trade?
The app doesn't block Moderate-confidence trades — it shows the score and highlights which rules aren't met. But Analytics tracks P&L separately for A+, Moderate, and Low. Most traders discover only A+ trades are profitable on average, making the choice clear over time.
F — Morning Mentor & Notifications
Q20 What is the Morning Mentor?
A daily AI push notification arriving before market open. It analyzes recent trading behavior (last 24–72h) and sends a personalized coaching message referencing your actual trades. Example:

"Good morning. Yesterday you placed 3 trades after your initial stop-out — each one larger than the last. That's your revenge trading pattern. Today, set a daily loss limit before the session starts."
Q21 When does the Morning Mentor arrive?
  • Forex: Before London or New York session open
  • Indian Market: Before 9:15 AM IST (NSE/BSE open)
  • Weekends: Sunday evening — week preparation message
Q22 Are notifications personalized to my actual trades?
Yes. Notifications reference your specific trades — a revenge trading alert says exactly how many trades you placed, after which loss, and by how much you oversized. This personalization is what makes them feel like coaching rather than spam.
G — Trading Setups
Q23 What is a "Trading Setup" in Edgecipline?
A named, documented strategy with defined rules and reference images. Once defined, it flows into the Checklist (for rule verification), Analytics (for performance tracking), and AI Reports (for coaching context). Examples: London Breakout, Nifty Opening Range, EMA Pullback.
Q24 Can I add reference images to setups?
Yes — up to 5 reference screenshots per setup showing ideal entries. During the checklist, these appear alongside rules so you can visually compare current market conditions against historical ideal setups.
H — Indian Market
Q25 Is Edgecipline built for Indian traders specifically?
Yes. The Indian market module was built from the ground up for NSE/BSE traders: full F&O support (strike/expiry), Intraday/Delivery/BTST types, ₹ analytics, 9:15 AM IST morning mentor, AI extraction trained on Indian broker screenshots, lot-size aware P&L calculation.
Q26 I trade both Nifty options and Forex. Can I use Edgecipline for both?
Yes. The Market Switcher in the header toggles between Forex and Indian Market modes. Both maintain completely separate journals, analytics, and setups. One subscription covers both markets.
Q27 Does the app work with Zerodha, Upstox, Groww?
Yes. AI extraction supports: Zerodha Kite, Upstox, Groww, Angel One, IIFL Markets, Dhan, Fyers, 5Paisa. Manual entry works for any broker regardless of screenshot support.
I — Subscription & Pricing
Q28 What happens to my data if I downgrade from Pro to Free?
Your data is never deleted. All trades, setups, and history remain intact. You lose access to Pro-gated features (AI extraction, full analytics, weekly reports, morning mentor) but all data is preserved and fully re-accessible when you re-subscribe.
Q29 Is annual worth it over monthly?
Annual Pro offers 30–40% savings. If you plan to use the app for more than 4–5 months, annual is the better value. Serious traders who integrate Edgecipline into their daily routine consistently renew annually.
Q30 Is there a plan for trading communities or prop firms?
Yes. Community and business licensing is available for Discord servers, Telegram groups, trading courses, and prop firms. Contact the team for group pricing and white-label options.
J — Privacy & Data Security
Q31 Is my trade data private? Who can see it?
Your data is private to your account. No other user can see your trades, analytics, or reports. Edgecipline does not sell or share individual trading data. The only opt-in exception is a future community leaderboard (discipline score only, no trade details).
Q32 Are my trade screenshots stored securely?
Yes. All images are stored in Cloudinary — enterprise-grade cloud storage with encryption at rest and in transit. Screenshots are linked only to your account.
Q33 Can I delete my account and all my data?
Yes. Request full account deletion from the Profile page or by contacting support. Deletion is permanent and irreversible.
K — Technical
Q34 I'm not receiving Morning Mentor notifications. How do I fix it?
  • Check notifications are enabled: Android Settings → Notifications → Edgecipline
  • Disable battery optimization for Edgecipline (kills background processes)
  • Confirm you have a Pro subscription (Morning Mentor is a Pro feature)
  • Ensure you've logged enough trades — the mentor needs data to analyze
  • Check Notifications page in-app — messages appear there even if push fails
Q35 I accidentally deleted a trade. Can I recover it?
Currently no — deleted trades cannot be recovered. This is intentional to prevent users from selectively deleting losing trades to game analytics. A confirmation prompt always appears before deletion.
Q36 Can I use Edgecipline on multiple devices?
Yes. Your account is cloud-based — log in on any device (Android, tablet, browser) and all data syncs automatically.
L — Product Philosophy
Q37 Will using Edgecipline make me profitable?
Edgecipline does not make trading decisions and does not guarantee profitability. It gives you accurate data about your own behavior and a coaching system to improve it. Traders who consistently log, use the checklist, and act on weekly insights typically see measurable improvement in discipline score and behavioral mistakes within 60–90 days. Profitability follows from discipline — but execution is always yours.
Q38 I already have a trading strategy. Why do I need this app?
Having a strategy and executing it consistently are completely different skills. Most traders with good strategies lose money not because their strategy is wrong, but because they don't follow it when it counts — when they're scared, greedy, or recovering from a loss.

Edgecipline's value is not in improving your strategy. It is in helping you follow the strategy you already have.
Q39 Does the app give trading signals or tell me when to buy/sell?
No. Edgecipline is not a signal service, copy-trading platform, or market analysis tool. It is a behavioral coaching and analytics platform. All trade decisions remain entirely yours.
Q40 How is the AI coaching generated — templates or real AI?
Real AI. Weekly reports and Morning Mentor messages are generated using large language models that receive your actual trade data as input. The AI reads your trades, identifies behavioral patterns, calculates impact, and writes a coaching response personalized to your specific situation. As your history grows, coaching becomes more precise.
Q41 What happens if I have a really bad trading week? Will the app make me feel worse?
No. The coaching philosophy is rooted in data, not judgment. A bad week is presented as patterns and numbers — not shame or failure. Weekly reports always include positive reinforcement alongside improvement areas. The morning mentor after a loss is supportive — focused on reset and preparation, not criticism. The app presents truth. It does not punish.
Q42 Is Edgecipline suitable for prop firm traders?
Yes — prop firm traders are one of the most motivated user segments. Key features for them:
  • Max drawdown tracking — shown prominently on Dashboard
  • Pre-trade checklist — prevents rule violations that fail evaluations
  • No stop-loss alerts — immediately flags missing risk management
  • Discipline score — auditable proof of rule adherence
  • Morning mentor — prevents tilt/revenge trading that ends evaluations
Q43 What is the long-term vision for Edgecipline?
To become the standard accountability and development platform for serious retail traders globally — the same way Strava became the standard for runners and cyclists.

Over time: broker integrations (auto-import), live market alerts, AI backtesting, a trader community, and an AI coach you can have a conversation with — one that knows your entire trading history and coaches you like a human mentor would.

The mission never changes: help every trader close the gap between knowing what to do and actually doing it.
