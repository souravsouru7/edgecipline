Best Patterns — Pattern Detection Engine
🔍
Pattern Detection Engine
Finds repeated condition combinations that produce better entries and stronger P&L.

Where Trading DNA looks at single variables (session OR mood), the Pattern Detection Engine finds combinations. It asks: "When you traded London + Confident + Breakout setup, what happened compared to London + Anxious + Breakout setup?" Two identical setups in the same session can produce opposite results depending on your state.

EXAMPLE — TOP DETECTED PATTERNS
Pattern (Combination) Occurrences Win Rate Avg P&L
London + Confident + Breakout 9 78% +$187
NY + Peak Mood + Trend Follow 7 71% +$134
London + Neutral + Reversal 8 50% +$12
Asian + Stressed + Any Setup 6 17% -$218
🔍 Pattern Insight
"London + Confident + Breakout" is Aryan's highest-edge pattern. It shows up 9 times with a 78% win rate. This is not a coincidence — it's a repeatable edge he can now intentionally prioritize. Meanwhile, "Asian + Stressed + Any Setup" is a pattern he should have a rule to avoid entirely.

Risk Pattern Detection
⚡
Risk Pattern Detection
Detects repeated loss clusters, tilt spirals, and dangerous condition combinations before they wipe your account.

A single bad trade isn't a pattern. But losing 3 trades in a row on Mondays, always after a green Friday, while trading the Asian session — that's a risk pattern. This engine detects exactly those clusters so you can add a rule before they repeat.

EXAMPLE — DETECTED RISK PATTERNS
⚠ TILT SPIRAL DETECTED
Pattern: After 2 consecutive losses, you have taken a 3rd trade within 15 minutes — 7 out of 7 times. That 3rd trade has a 0% win rate and an average loss of -$143.
Rule suggestion: No trading for 30 mins after 2 consecutive losses.
⚠ MONDAY LOSS CLUSTER
Pattern: Mondays account for 41% of your total losses despite representing only 20% of your trading days. Win rate on Monday: 28% vs your overall 54%.
Rule suggestion: Observe-only on Mondays until market direction is confirmed by Tuesday.
⚡ SIZE ESCALATION AFTER WINS
Pattern: After 3 consecutive wins, your average lot size increases by 2.3×. Your win rate on those oversized trades drops to 38% — suggesting confidence becomes overconfidence.
Rule suggestion: Cap lot size at baseline regardless of streak length.

What Should I Improve? — Self Awareness & Discipline Analytics
🪞
Self Awareness Engine — Review Calibration
Measures how accurately you rate your own trades vs how they actually performed.

After every trade you mark: "Would I retake this trade?" The Self Awareness engine compares your answer to the actual outcome. If you said "Yes, good trade" but it lost — that's poor calibration. If you said "No, bad process" and it won by luck — that's also poor calibration. The goal is accurate self-assessment, not just winning.

REVIEW CALIBRATION MATRIX
Self ReviewActual OutcomeCountWhat It Means
✅ Good process Win 14 Perfectly calibrated
❌ Bad process Loss 9 Correctly identified mistake
✅ Good process Loss 7 Good process, bad luck (fine)
❌ Bad process Win 4 ⚠ Won for wrong reasons — danger zone
🪞 Calibration Insight
This trader has 4 "bad process wins" — trades where they broke their rules but profited anyway. These are the most dangerous trades in the journal: they reinforce bad habits by accidentally rewarding them. The Self Awareness Engine flags these so you can consciously reject the false positive.

One specific, evidence-backed action pulled from your actual trading data — not generic advice.

Generic trading advice is everywhere. The AI Coach Feed only shows you insights derived from your data. Each card shows: observation, evidence (which trades), action (what to do), and expected outcome (what will change).

HIGH PRIORITY
Revenge Trading
Risk Management

Pre-Trade Checklist — Convert Insight into Execution
Run your setup rules before entering a trade. Intelligence becomes a real behavior, not just a chart.

Every insight in the Intelligence Hub is only valuable if it changes your actual behavior. The Pre-Trade Checklist is where analysis meets execution. Before you click Buy/Sell, you run through your own rules — the ones you built from your own data.

EXAMPLE CHECKLIST (ARYAN'S RULES BUILT FROM HIS DNA)
  • ✓
    Session check: Am I in London Open or NY session? (Asian session → skip trade)
  • ✓
    Mood check: Is my mood 4 or 5 out of 5? (3 or below → reduce size by 50% or skip)
  • ✓
    Tilt check: Have I lost 2 or more trades already today? (Yes → close platform for 30 min before re-entering)
  • ✓
    Setup confirmation: Does this match my Breakout or Trend Follow setup criteria?
  • ✓
    SL check: Is my stop loss placed before I enter? (No SL = no entry)
  • ✓
    Size check: Is my lot size at baseline regardless of my current win streak?
  • ✗
    FOMO check: Am I entering because price is already moving? (Yes → wait for the next setup)
✅ Why the Checklist Matters
Most traders know their rules but break them in the moment. The checklist turns knowledge into a physical gate. Traders who run a pre-trade checklist consistently show +22–34% improvement in rule adherence within 4 weeks. Your discipline score tracks this — so you can see the improvement in the Discipline Analytics over time.
